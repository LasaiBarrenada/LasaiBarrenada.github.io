Hi, I'm Lasai.

I'm a medical statistician dedicated to developing, validating, and improving clinical prediction models to aid medical decision-making.

I'm a final-year PhD student at KU Leuven in the Development and Regeneration department and within the International Ovarian Tumour Analysis (IOTA) group, supervised by Ben Van Calster, Laure Wynants, and Dirk Timmerman. My work combines methodological and applied research to improve the calibration and transportability of clinical prediction models, with a focus on the ADNEX model for ovarian cancer diagnosis.

If you'd like to collaborate or have questions about my work, feel free to reach out: lasai.barrenadataleb@kuleuven.be
